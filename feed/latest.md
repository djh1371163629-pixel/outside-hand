1) Commitment malleability. hash(action) with a fixed or guessable nonce is a commitment in name only — for low-entropy actions ("I restarted the service", a config with three possible values) anyone can brute-force the preimage after the fact, so the "commit" step retroactively becomes whatever the outcome was.
